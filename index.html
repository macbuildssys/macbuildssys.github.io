I am a security engineer specialising in DevSecOps and product security. My work covers software supply chain security, vulnerability scanning, assessment and management, and application security. I am currently studying for a Master's degree in Advanced Computer Science (Cybersecurity) and am particularly interested in the use of AI for access management.

Background

My professional journey has taken me through several distinct fields before leading me to Cybersecurity. Starting out in customer relationship management systems, I then moved into telecommunications before eventually finding my footing in Cybersecurity. This unusual route has given me a well rounded, almost restless curiosity about how organisations actually function beneath their processes and policies. That curiosity turned into a specialism: software supply chain security, vulnerability assessment and management and application security. These are the quieter disciplines that keep systems trustworthy long after they have been shipped. I am now building on this foundation by studying for an MSc Advanced Computer Science with a focus on Cybersecurity at the University of Hertfordshire. So far, I have excelled in the following modules:

  • Advanced Algorithms and Paradigms
  • Distributed Systems Security
  • Digital Forensics
  • Penetration Testing

My focus area lies at the edge of that discipline, in how AI algorithms and access management might be brought closer together to build systems that respond more intelligently to context.

Open source

In my leisure time, I maintain a handful of security and system tools. Filter them by the kind of problem they tackle.

  • mime-filter

    Brings the MIME type and content signature verification used in email servers, secure web gateways and firewalls to the browser itself, applying that same layer of defence to everyday downloads.

    Application security, Privacy

  • LocalChat

    A chat interface for language models that run on your own computer through Ollama. Your conversations never leave your machine, there is no account to create and no per message bill. It puts secure local AI inference into practice for anyone who wants to use AI on private material.

    Privacy

  • ConsentGuard

    An Android app that refuses cookie and consent pop ups for you, in your browser and inside other apps. Instead of tapping a single "Reject All" button, it switches off every individual consent toggle, because some sites only let you object that way. It is built on Android's AccessibilityService, so it is not tied to any one browser.

    Privacy

  • vulnscope

    An ML pipeline analysing NVD supply chain CVE trends from 2015 to 2026, using linear regression, logistic regression and random forest models to forecast emerging weakness categories.

    Supply chain

  • dockerfile-hardener

    A CLI tool that analyses Dockerfiles for security misconfigurations, applies auto fixes where possible, and produces reports in table, JSON, SARIF and HTML formats, covering ten hardening rules.

    Supply chain, Application security

  • secret-scanner

    Combines regex pattern matching with Shannon entropy scoring to detect hardcoded credentials while suppressing false positives, outputting SARIF 2.1.0 for GitHub's Security tab.

    Supply chain, Application security

  • spectre-meltdown

    Parses spectre-meltdown-checker output into a structured HTML report, mapping CPU level exposure to kernel mitigation status across Spectre, Meltdown, MDS and related microarchitectural side channel issues.

    Systems

  • api-sec-scanner

    A proof of concept vulnerability scanner for HTTPS and REST APIs, built to showcase automated detection techniques and serve as a foundation for further research.

    Application security

  • mac-shell

    A Unix shell built from scratch in Rust, written to understand how shells actually work under the hood.

    Systems
