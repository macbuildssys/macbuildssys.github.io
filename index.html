About Me

Background

My professional journey has taken me through several distinct fields before leading me to cybersecurity. Starting out in customer relationship management systems, I then moved into telecommunications before eventually finding my footing in cybersecurity. This unusual route has given me a well-rounded, almost restless curiosity about how organisations actually function beneath their processes and policies. That curiosity turned into a specialism: software supply chain security, vulnerability management and application security. These are the quieter disciplines that keep systems trustworthy long after they have been shipped. I am now building on this foundation by studying for an MSc Advanced Computer Science with a focus on cybersecurity at the University of Hertfordshire. So far, I have excelled in the following modules:

  1. Advanced Algorithms and Paradigms
  2. Distributed Systems Security
  3. Machine Learning
  4. Penetration Testing

My focus area lies at the edge of that discipline, in how AI algorithms and access management might be brought closer together to build systems that respond more intelligently to context.

Projects

Open-source

In my leisure time, I maintain a handful of security and system tools.

mime-filter

Brings the MIME-type and content-signature verification used in email servers, secure web gateways and firewalls to the browser itself, applying that same layer of defence to everyday downloads.

LocalChat

A fully offline LLM interface for your own Ollama models. No cloud, no subscriptions, no API costs, just local AI on your own terms.

vulnscope

An ML pipeline analysing NVD supply-chain CVE trends from 2015 to 2026, using linear regression, logistic regression and random forest models to forecast emerging weakness categories.

dockerfile-hardener

A CLI tool that analyses Dockerfiles for security misconfigurations, applies auto-fixes where possible, and produces reports in table, JSON, SARIF and HTML formats, covering ten hardening rules.

secret-scanner

Combines regex pattern matching with Shannon entropy scoring to detect hardcoded credentials while suppressing false positives, outputting SARIF 2.1.0 for GitHub's Security tab.

spectre-meltdown

Parses spectre-meltdown-checker output into a structured HTML report, mapping CPU-level exposure to kernel mitigation status across Spectre, Meltdown, MDS and related microarchitectural side-channel issues.
